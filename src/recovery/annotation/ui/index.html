    1 · Locate the Failure

    Select an action on the left, then use the buttons above. Root Cause and Clear Failure are separate judgments and may refer to the same action.
    Root cause Not selected
    Root Cause: an action that sent the trajectory off course and was not later corrected by the agent. Do not count a recoverable mistake the agent successfully fixes; prefer the most fundamental cause over a later symptom.
    Clear failure Not selected
    Clear Failure (Identifiable): the step where the screenshot or trace provides enough evidence to determine that the overall task has failed.

    5 · Clean Prefix Review

    In the left sidebar, select the contiguous detour from step x through the step immediately before recovery, then click Delete. The next retained row becomes the recovery step x+n. Original step IDs never change. Do not delete persistent or causally relevant actions.
